Documents You'll Need

Keep these documents handy for a smooth travel experience

Aadhaar Card — Government issued ID proof (mandatory)
Passport — Required for international trips and some domestic flights
PAN Card — For payment and tax purposes
Passport Size Photos — 2-4 recent photographs (white background)
Visa — Required for international travel (if applicable)
COVID Certificate — Vaccination certificate (optional, some places may require)
Emergency Contact Number — Family or friend's contact for emergencies
